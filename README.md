Rate yourself across the 9 pillars and the app plots your radar, derives a career level, and shows where you sit against a 45-point competency matrix spanning 5 proficiency levels.

Everything runs in the browser. There is no backend and no account: your profile is saved to `localStorage` and never leaves your machine.
